What We Do

Complete Landscaping Services Across Southampton

Whether you need a full garden redesign or seasonal maintenance, we handle every aspect of outdoor space creation and care.

Garden Design & Planning
Custom layouts tailored to your budget and lifestyle. We assess drainage, sunlight, and soil to create a garden that thrives for years.
Patio & Hardscaping
Porcelain, natural stone, and block paving installations. Perfectly levelled surfaces with integrated drainage—built to last.
Fencing & Boundaries
Timber, composite, and metal fencing installed to specifications. We handle removal of old panels and post repairs too.
Planting & Borders
Strategic plant selection for colour, structure, and low-maintenance appeal. Includes soil preparation and seasonal mulching.
Garden Maintenance
Regular lawn care, hedge trimming, weeding, and seasonal tidy-ups. Keep your garden looking sharp without the weekend chore.
Decking & Structures
Bespoke garden decks, raised beds, and pergolas. Built with treated timber to withstand the New Forest climate.

Our Story

Ashley Landscaping was born from a simple frustration: watching neighbours settle for mediocre garden work at inflated prices. For over 15 years, we've designed and built outdoor spaces across Southampton and the wider South Coast, working directly with homeowners to turn rough plots into thriving, usable gardens. Our approach combines practical horticulture with honest project management—no upselling, no corner-cutting.

We've earned our reputation through reliability and attention to detail. Most of our work comes from repeat customers and personal referrals, because people trust us to deliver what we promise. Whether you need a complete garden overhaul or regular seasonal care, we treat your outdoor space with the same care we'd give our own homes.
