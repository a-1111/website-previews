Our Story

Andy Watson Plumbing & Gas Engineering has served Guildford and the surrounding areas for over 15 years. We're a Gas Safe Registered engineer with a reputation built on turning up on time, fixing problems right the first time, and treating your home with respect. From emergency midnight burst pipes to planned boiler installations, we've handled thousands of jobs and earned the trust of families and businesses across the town.

We don't charge call-out fees and we don't push expensive solutions you don't need. Most of our work comes from repeat customers and personal recommendations because we keep things straightforward and fair. Whether it's a quick tap repair or a full heating system overhaul, you get the same attention to detail and professionalism every time.
